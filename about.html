About RHEEMA

A brand by Ranpa Exim, crafted for homes that value thoughtful design.

The Brand

Thoughtful essentials for modern Indian homes

RHEEMA is the consumer brand of Ranpa Exim — born from a simple observation: the most-used objects in a home deserve the most thought. We design everyday essentials that are beautiful to look at, honest in material, and effortless to use.

Our journey began with the kitchen — the heart of the Indian home. The 6 Grid Rotating Food Dispenser is our flagship: six separate compartments, a smooth 360° rotation, and one-touch dispensing that keeps rice, lentils, beans and cereals fresh, visible and within reach.

How We Work

From idea to your kitchen

Observe

We start in real kitchens, watching how families store, reach and cook with their staples every single day.

Design

Every product is sketched, prototyped and refined until it feels obvious — food-grade materials, precise mechanisms, finishes worth showing off.

Test

Nothing carries the RHEEMA name until it's been tested thoroughly in daily use. If it doesn't make life calmer, it doesn't ship.

Deliver

From our facility to your countertop — packed with care, supported with a warranty, and always one WhatsApp message away.

The RHEEMA Promise
Founder's View
A calmer home begins with the details.
"I started RHEEMA because everyday products should do more than serve a function — they should bring clarity, confidence and a sense of calm to the way we live. Our first product, the 6 Grid Rotating Food Dispenser, responds to a routine we see every day: several staple ingredients competing for limited kitchen space. By making them easier to see, reach and dispense, we can remove a small point of friction from cooking. This product is only the beginning. The vision is to create a trusted collection of considered essentials, made with care for modern living." Founder, RHEEMA
