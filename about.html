Our Story

About RHEEMA

A brand by Ranpa Exim, crafted for homes that value thoughtful design.

The Brand

RHEEMA is the consumer brand of Ranpa Exim — born from a simple observation: the most-used objects in a home deserve the most thought. We design everyday essentials that are beautiful to look at, honest in material, and effortless to use.

Our journey began with the kitchen — the heart of the Indian home. The 6 Grid Rotating Food Dispenser is our flagship: six separate compartments, a smooth 360° rotation, and one-touch dispensing that keeps rice, lentils, beans and cereals fresh, visible and within reach.

"Organized Today. Peace of Mind Everyday." The RHEEMA Promise

Founder's View

Good design should feel obvious once it exists. We started RHEEMA because we believed Indian homes deserved storage that works as beautifully as it looks — food-grade materials, precise mechanisms, and finishes you'd be proud to keep on the counter, not hidden in a cupboard.

Every RHEEMA product is chosen slowly and tested thoroughly before it carries our name. If it doesn't make daily life calmer, it doesn't ship.

Good design should feel obvious once it exists.Founder, RHEEMA
