How to Use It

Using the 6 Grid Rotating Food Dispenser takes four simple steps. Once the compartments are filled, daily cooking becomes a matter of rotating, pressing and collecting — no scooping, no opening six jars.

  1. Fill each compartment. Open the top lid and fill each of the six compartments with one dry ingredient, such as rice in one and toor dal in another. Keep ingredients dry before filling.
  2. Close the lid. Close the top lid so the sealed design helps keep moisture and dust out.
  3. Place a bowl and press the button. Place a bowl under the outlet drawer and press the button to dispense. Collect what you need, then press again to stop.
  4. Rotate to the next compartment. Turn the drum to bring the next compartment to the front, and dispense again.
