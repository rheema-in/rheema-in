Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

When you contact us or place an order, we collect:

  • Your name, phone number and email address

  • Order details and payment information
  • Messages you send us via WhatsApp, email or the contact form

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your orders
  • To respond to your enquiries and provide customer support
  • To send order updates (confirmation, dispatch, delivery)
  • To improve our products and website

We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

3. WhatsApp Communication

Our ordering works through WhatsApp. Messages you send are subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy in addition to ours.

4. Cookies & Analytics

This site may use basic cookies and analytics to understand visitor traffic. No advertising trackers are used.

5. Data Sharing

We share your delivery details only with courier/shipping partners to fulfil your order, and with payment providers to process payments. We do not share your data for marketing by third parties.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

7. Your Rights

You may ask us at any time to view, correct or delete your personal information. Contact us at info.rheema@gmail.com or +91 73829 26533.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page.
